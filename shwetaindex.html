About & Research Interests

I am an academician and researcher with a strong background in Computer Science, specializing in Cyber Security and Machine Learning. My Ph.D. research focused on the empirical detection and prevention of E-mail Phishing. Currently, I serve as an Assistant Professor at Maitreyi College, University of Delhi, where I am dedicated to fostering the next generation of tech professionals while actively contributing to the global research community through interdisciplinary studies in IoT security, health informatics, and predictive modeling.


Core Research Areas:

Cyber Security Machine Learning & AI Phishing Detection IoT Security Health Informatics Data Mining
